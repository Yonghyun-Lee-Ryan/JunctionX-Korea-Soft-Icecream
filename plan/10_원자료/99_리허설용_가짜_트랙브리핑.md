## 왜 3개인가

`/jx-track`은 **트랙마다 병렬**로 돌게 만들었고, 22:20~22:25의 「판정 규칙 대입」은 **비교할 대상이 있어야** 연습이 된다. 트랙 하나로는 그 5분을 리허설할 수 없다.

세 트랙은 [[04_사전결정]] D-2의 판정 규칙이 **실제로 걸러내는지**를 시험하도록 일부러 다르게 만들었다.

## 쓰는 법

1. 팀 4명이 모인다. **아래 브리핑은 시작 전까지 아무도 읽지 않는다**

## Track A — Upstage

```
[FICTIONAL — REHEARSAL ONLY]

## Structured Decisions from Unstructured Documents

### Who we are
Upstage builds Solar LLM and Document AI. Our Document Parse and
Information Extract APIs are used by financial institutions, law firms,
and public agencies across Korea to turn paperwork into data.

### The challenge
Korean organizations still run on documents that were designed to be
read by humans: scanned contracts, hand-stamped approval forms, PDF
reports where the meaningful number lives inside a merged table cell.

Extraction is largely a solved problem. Deciding is not.

Our customers tell us the same thing repeatedly: they can now extract
the fields, but they still route every document to a person, because
nobody trusts a pipeline that cannot say why it produced an answer, or
admit that it is unsure.

Build something that closes the gap between extraction and a decision
someone is willing to act on.

### What we want to see
- A workflow where a document goes in and a decision, recommendation,
  or routing action comes out
- An honest treatment of uncertainty. We would rather see a system that
  says "I am not confident about this clause, here is why" than one that
  claims 99% accuracy
- Traceability: for any output, show the exact region of the source
  document it came from

### What we are not looking for
- OCR accuracy benchmarks. We already have those numbers
- A chat interface on top of a PDF. This exists
- Document search. Also exists

### Resources
- Solar LLM API credits (₩200,000 per team)
- Document Parse / Information Extract API access
- A sample corpus of 500 de-identified Korean business documents
  (contracts, invoices, internal approval forms)

### Evaluation
40% — Does the output actually support a decision?
30% — Is the uncertainty handling honest and visible?
20% — Technical execution
10% — Demo clarity

Teams must use at least one Upstage API. Mentors will be at the
Upstage desk throughout Saturday.
```

## Track B — Microsoft

```
[FICTIONAL — REHEARSAL ONLY]

## Agents That Fit the Way People Already Work

### Who we are
Microsoft Azure AI. We provide Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI Service,
and the agent tooling that sits on top of them.

### The challenge
Most "AI agents" assume the user will change their behaviour to suit the
agent: open a new app, learn a new prompt style, adopt a new workflow.

Adoption data says otherwise. The tools that stick are the ones that
meet people inside the software, the language, and the rhythm they
already use.

We want to see agents that reduce the distance between what someone is
already doing and what the agent does for them.

### Directions we find interesting
- Agents that operate inside an existing surface rather than beside it
- Agents that observe a repeated manual process and offer to take it over,
  with the person keeping the final call
- Agents for people who are usually designed for last: users with limited
  bandwidth, older devices, low digital confidence, or a disability

### What we are not looking for
- A wrapper that forwards a prompt to a model
- An agent that requires the user to describe their task in perfect detail
  before it does anything
- Autonomy without a visible undo

### Resources
- Azure credits ($300 per team)
- Azure OpenAI Service (GPT-family models), Azure AI Foundry agent tooling
- Azure Speech, Vision, and Translator APIs

### Evaluation
35% — Does it reduce real friction for a real person?
25% — Quality of the human-in-the-loop design
25% — Technical execution on Azure
15% — Demo

Teams must deploy at least one component on Azure. Azure engineers
will hold office hours on Saturday.
```

## Track C — Hanmirae Logistics *(가상 회사)*

```
[FICTIONAL — REHEARSAL ONLY. This company does not exist.]

## Predictive Load Balancing for Regional Distribution

### Who we are
Hanmirae Logistics operates 14 regional distribution centres and a fleet
of 2,300 vehicles across Korea.

### The challenge
Our regional centres are unevenly loaded. A centre can sit at 40%
capacity on a Tuesday while a centre 60km away is turning trucks away.
Rebalancing decisions are currently made by regional managers using
experience and a spreadsheet.

Build a system that predicts imbalance before it happens and proposes
reallocation across centres.

### What we want to see
- Forecasting that uses our historical throughput data
- Reallocation proposals a regional manager could actually execute
- Quantified impact: how much idle capacity does your approach recover?

### Resources
- 3 years of anonymised throughput data across 14 centres (CSV, 4.2M rows)
- Vehicle routing distance matrix
- No API credits provided

### Evaluation
40% — Forecasting accuracy against a held-out test period
30% — Practicality of the reallocation proposals
20% — Technical execution
10% — Presentation

Grand prize for this track: ₩5,000,000 (largest single track prize)
```

## 리허설 뒤에 열 것 — 설계 의도

> [!note]- 🔴 리허설을 끝내기 전에는 열지 마라
>
> **세 트랙은 판정 규칙이 실제로 걸러내는지 시험하려고 만들었다.** 정답을 정해두진 않았지만, 각 트랙이 어느 규칙에 걸리게 설계했는지는 이렇다.
>
> | 판정 규칙 | A (Upstage) | B (Microsoft) | C (Hanmirae) |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 당사자가 이 건물 안에 있나 | △ 기업 실무자 — 현장엔 드물다 | ✅ **참가자 본인이 당사자가 될 수 있다** | ❌ 지역 물류 관리자. 현장에 없다 |
> | 데모 화면이 나오나 | ✅ 문서 → 근거 표시 | ✅ 기존 화면 안에서 동작 | ❌ 예측 대시보드뿐 |
> | 처음 쓰는 기술 1개 이하 | 팀 스택에 달림 | 팀 스택에 달림 | ⚠️ 시계열 예측을 안 해봤으면 위험 |
> | 파트너 API를 자연스럽게 쓰나 | ✅ 필수 명시 | ✅ 필수 명시 | ❌ 크레딧 없음 |
>
> **C가 함정이다.** 상금이 가장 크고(₩5,000,000) 데이터가 풍부해서 매력적으로 보이지만 —
> - 당사자가 현장에 없어 [[00_수집가이드]]의 현장 조달 전략이 안 먹힌다. **2시간 안에 근거를 못 만든다**
> - 배점 40%가 예측 정확도인데, 그건 48시간에 **다른 팀도 다 하는 것**이고 우리가 이길 근거가 없다
> - 데모가 대시보드라 Expo 3시간에서 불리하다
>
> **리허설에서 확인할 것: 우리 규칙이 C를 걸러냈나?** 상금 크기에 끌려 C로 갔다면 판정 규칙이 약한 것이다 → [[04_사전결정]] D-2를 고친다.
>
> **A와 B 사이에서 갈렸다면 그게 정상이다.** 둘 다 합리적 선택이고, 이 리허설의 목적은 정답 맞히기가 아니라 **2시간 안에 결정이 나는지**를 보는 것이다.
>
> ### 결정 말고 관찰할 것
